## Philosophy

Most personal inventory trackers are either dry spreadsheets or generic productivity apps that lack spirit. **Vessel** approaches your possessions, daily rituals, and personal growth with the aesthetic gravitas of dark fantasy RPGs (*Dishonored*, *The Elder Scrolls*, and gothic arcane ledgers).

Every physical artifact you own confers real-world attribute modifiers. Every completed prayer, workout rep, or shipped feature awards experience to your character sheet.

---

## Core Systems

### 1. The Armory & Visual Wardrobe (System 1)
- **Interactive Paperdoll Loadout**: Equippable silhouette slots (`Headwear`, `Outerwear`, `Base Layer`, `Trousers`, `Footwear`, `Tech Relic`, `Tome / Grimoire`, and `Wrist / EDC`).
- **Dynamic Attribute Buffs**: Wearing gear confers real-life modifiers (e.g. *Canvas Overshirt: +3 Style, +2 Comfort*; *ANC Headphones: +6 Focus*; *Noble Qur'an: +10 WIS*).
- **Weight & Encumbrance Meter**: Prevents closet bloat by calculating total physical load against your maximum carry capacity.
- **Rarity & Condition Integrity**: Inspect durability (`Pristine`, `Seasoned`, `Worn`, `Needs Repair`), color swatches, and seasonal tags.
- **Locked Loot (Wishlist / Black Market)**: Track items you aspire to acquire in the future, locked behind specific real-world discipline milestones (e.g. *"Unlock after 30-day Salah streak + shipping Project X"*).

### 2. The Character Codex & Skill Constellations (System 2)
The Four Pillars of Life Progression:
- **STR (The Flesh / Body)**: Bodyweight volume, pushup/squat streaks, posture integrity, and stamina.
- **INT (The Artificer / Craft)**: Engineering velocity, deep work focus, design mastery, and shipped milestones.
- **WIS (The Soul / Spiritual)**: Circadian Salah discipline, Surah memorization, and inner serenity (*Sakinah*).
- **CHA (The Circle / Social)**: 1-on-1 mentorship, community enablement, and dialectic leadership.

**Skyrim-Style Constellations**:
Ascension nodes unlock across Tiers 1 through 3 as you pass real habit milestones (e.g. *Kinetic Foundation*, *Architectonic Thinking*, *Five Pillars Cadence*, *Servant Leader*).

### 3. Daily Rituals & Circadian Cadence (System 3)
- **The Five Pillars (Salah)**: Five runic stones for `Al-Fajr`, `Al-Dhuhr`, `Al-Asr`, `Al-Maghrib`, and `Al-Isha` that ignite with glowing runes upon completion, awarding WIS XP and daily covenant bonuses.
- **Kinetic Rep Volume**: Instant counters for pushups and squats with real-time STR gains.
- **Deep Work Tracker**: Unbroken focus sprint logger awarding INT XP.
- **Soul & Kindred Notes**: Revision logs for Surahs and mentorship logs for peers.

### 4. Arcane Audio Synthesizer
- Built 100% on native browser **Web Audio API** (zero external mp3 assets or network requests).
- Features stone/brass equipment clinks, parchment clicks, and harmonic arcane chords upon milestone completion. Fully mutable with a single click.

### 5. Local-First & Sovereign Data
- 100% offline-ready.
- Auto-syncs to browser `LocalStorage`.
- One-click JSON backup export and restore to ensure your life ledger is private and permanent.
